01

The Most Neglected Investment 最被忽视的一笔投资

You spend about one-third of your life sleeping, yet most people treat sleep as if it were optional. Research by Dr. Matthew Walker, a neuroscientist at UC Berkeley, shows that sleep is one of the most important investments you can make in your health—and one of the most neglected.

你一生中大约有三分之一的时间在睡觉，但大多数人却把睡眠当作可有可无的事。加州大学伯克利分校神经科学家 Matthew Walker 博士的研究表明：睡眠是你能为自己的健康做出的最重要投资之一，同时也是最被忽视的一项。

02

The Cost of Sleep Deprivation 睡眠不足的代价

The consequences of sleep deprivation are severe. A single night of poor sleep can reduce your immune function by up to 70 percent, making you more vulnerable to infections. Chronic sleep deprivation is linked to obesity, diabetes, heart disease, Alzheimer's disease, and depression.

睡眠不足的后果相当严重。仅仅一晚没睡好，就可能让免疫功能最多下降 70%，使你更容易被感染。而长期睡眠不足，则与肥胖、糖尿病、心脏病、阿尔茨海默病和抑郁症相关联。

03

What Your Brain Does While You Sleep 睡着之后，大脑在做什么

During sleep, your brain performs critical maintenance tasks. It consolidates memories—transferring information from short-term memory to long-term storage. It clears out toxic waste products that accumulate during the day through a system called the glymphatic system. It also processes emotions, which is why a good night's sleep can help you feel more emotionally balanced.

睡眠期间，大脑在进行关键的维护工作。它会巩固记忆——把信息从短期记忆转入长期存储；它会通过被称为「类淋巴系统（glymphatic system）」的机制，清除白天累积的毒性代谢废物；它还会处理情绪，这正是睡个好觉能让你情绪更平稳的原因。

04

Deep Sleep and REM Sleep 深睡与 REM，各司其职

Different stages of sleep serve different purposes. Deep sleep physically restores your body—it repairs tissues, builds bone and muscle, and strengthens the immune system. REM sleep, on the other hand, is essential for creativity, problem-solving, and emotional regulation. A full night's sleep includes multiple cycles of both deep sleep and REM sleep.

不同的睡眠阶段承担不同的任务。深睡负责身体的物理修复——修复组织、构建骨骼与肌肉、增强免疫系统；而 REM 睡眠则对创造力、问题解决与情绪调节至关重要。一整夜的睡眠里，深睡与 REM 会多次交替出现。

05

How Much — and When 睡多久，以及什么时候睡

Dr. Walker recommends getting seven to nine hours of sleep per night for adults. He also recommends maintaining a consistent sleep schedule—going to bed and waking up at the same time every day, even on weekends. This helps regulate your body's internal clock, also known as the circadian rhythm.

Walker 博士建议成年人每晚睡 7 到 9 小时。他还建议保持规律的作息——每天在同一时间上床、同一时间起床，周末也不例外。这有助于调节你体内的生物钟，也就是昼夜节律（circadian rhythm）。

06

Practical Strategies 四个可以马上执行的策略

Several practical strategies can improve sleep quality. Avoid caffeine after 2 p.m., keep your bedroom cool and dark, limit screen time before bed, and avoid alcohol before sleep—alcohol may help you fall asleep faster, but it disrupts REM sleep and reduces overall sleep quality.

有几个实用策略可以改善睡眠质量：下午 2 点之后不再摄入咖啡因；保持卧室凉爽、昏暗；睡前限制屏幕使用；睡前避免饮酒——酒精也许能让你更快入睡，但它会打断 REM 睡眠，降低整体睡眠质量。

07

Sleep Is Not a Luxury 结语：睡眠不是奢侈品

The key insight is that sleep is not a luxury—it is a necessity.

最核心的一点是：睡眠不是奢侈品，而是必需品。

“

The shorter your sleep, the shorter your life.

睡得越少，寿命越短。

Dr. Matthew Walker · UC Berkeley
